“"I had to wait to work because I couldn't take courses or do anything else since I had been identified in Croatia. I should have returned to Croatia. I entered the ICS reception centre and now I work in a pizzeria. They pay me very little: I have a contract for three hours a day for 600 euros, but I work many more hours and they pay me 400 euros.
